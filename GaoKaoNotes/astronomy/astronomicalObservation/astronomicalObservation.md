## Celestial Sphere

#### Celetial Poles
We have **North Celestial Pole** and **South Celestial Pole**. The definition of poles is the points of earth's rotational axis points to at celestial sphere. If Earth's rotation axis were perfectly fixed relative to the distant stars, then we have fixed celestial poles, of course. However, our Earth's rotation axis slowly precesses, which means its direction in space slowly changes(actually circular around a point)
